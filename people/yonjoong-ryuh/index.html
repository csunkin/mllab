Yonjoong Ryuh, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor

California State University - Northridge

Dr. Yonjoong Ryuh is an assistant professor in the Department of Kinesiology at California State University, Northridge. Before joining CSUN in 2026, he was an assistant professor of kinesiology at Sonoma State University from 2020 to 2026. He earned his Ph.D. in Exercise Science at Mississippi State University, with an emphasis on motor learning and intellectual disability, and his M.S. and B.S. at Seoul National University.

His research focuses on motor skill acquisition and learning approaches that reduce reliance on cognitive function, including implicit practice, errorless practice, and external focus of attention, with golf as a primary sport. He also studies ways to promote physical activity among individuals with intellectual disabilities.

Current Research Projects

Effects of Errorless Practice on Golf Putting Skill Acquisition and Movement Variability

Compares errorless and errorful practice to examine their effects on novice golfers' putting skill acquisition and movement variability after 24 hours.
