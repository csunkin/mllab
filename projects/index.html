Development and psychometric evaluation of the KinderKinetics Assessment Tool

Ovande Furtado Jr., Ph.D.

To collect validity and reliability evidence for the KinderKinetics Assessment Tool (KKAT). KKAT is an AI-based instrument designed to assess the motor skills of children. It employs computer vision and machine learning techniques to analyze children's movements during various motor tasks, providing objective and accurate assessments of their motor skill proficiency.
