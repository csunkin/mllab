Dr. Yeonhak Jung is an assistant professor in the Department of Kinesiology at California State University, Northridge. He earned his Ph.D. at The University of Texas at Austin, specializing in physical education teacher education, and his M.S. and B.S. at Korea University.

His research examines how physical activity, motor skill acquisition, and cognitive engagement relate to cognition, from children in schools to adults across the lifespan. Current work includes a community-engaged evaluation of a school-based motor skill intervention, service learning on health-related fitness in underserved communities, and fNIRS studies of cognitive responses to different types of exercise.
