Dr. Alexander Kurtzman is an assistant professor in the Department of Kinesiology at California State University, Northridge. He earned his Ph.D. in Physical Education, Exercise and Sports Science at the University of New Mexico and his M.S. in Physical Education Teaching at the University of Wisconsin-La Crosse. He holds a B.S. in Sport and Exercise Science from the University of Northern Colorado.

His research includes physical education teacher education, pathways into faculty careers, and professional development in higher education. His work also includes adventure education and teaching practices in physical education.
