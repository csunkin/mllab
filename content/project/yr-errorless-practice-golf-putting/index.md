This study will compare errorless and errorful practice to examine their effects on novice golfers' putting skill acquisition and movement variability after 24 hours. By maintaining a high likelihood of success, errorless practice may develop consistent movement patterns earlier while reducing the cognitive demands for error correction. Findings may inform motor learning approaches for people who benefit from reduced cognitive demands, including children, older adults, and people with intellectual disabilities.

The project team includes nine undergraduate researchers who meet biweekly.
